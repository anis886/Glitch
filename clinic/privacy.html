Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2024

1. Information We Collect

At Pearl Dental Clinic, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting all medical and personal data. When booking an appointment or contacting us, we collect details such as your full name, phone number, email address, appointment preferences, and health notes provided voluntarily by you.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is exclusively used for managing appointment scheduling, sending confirmation reminders (via SMS, WhatsApp, or Email), providing clinical care, and complying with Kuwait Ministry of Health record-keeping regulations.

3. Confidentiality & Security

We enforce strict encryption and access control protocols. We will never sell, trade, or expose patient data to third parties without your explicit written consent, except where required by Kuwait law.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding your data or wish to request data deletion, please contact our privacy officer at info@pearldental.com.kw.
