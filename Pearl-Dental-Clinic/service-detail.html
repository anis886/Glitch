Teeth Whitening

Professional in-clinic whitening for a brighter, radiant smile.

Overview

Our professional teeth whitening treatments are designed to safely remove stubborn stains caused by coffee, tea, smoking, and aging. Using medical-grade whitening agents activated by specialized light technology, we deliver noticeable results of up to 8 shades brighter in a single comfortable 60-minute visit.

Key Benefits

  • Fast 60-minute treatment duration
  • Safe for tooth enamel with minimal sensitivity
  • Dramatically removes deep discolored stains
  • Includes custom take-home maintenance kit
